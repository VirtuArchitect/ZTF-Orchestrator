ZTF-Orchestrator v1.6.0

Image: ghcr.io/virtuarchitect/ztf-orchestrator:v1.6.0

Highlights:
- Added read-only Nutanix Upgrade Advisor for pre-upgrade risk assessment.
- Added bundled upgrade guardrail rules for LCM prechecks, release-note review,
  compatibility review, Prism Central context, dark-site bundle review,
  Community Edition review, and AOS major-version planning.
- Added curated Source Packs for customer-owned KB summaries, release-note
  findings, support-case notes, lab findings, and internal upgrade standards.
- Added Upgrade Advisor evidence export with assessment JSON and Markdown.
- Preserved left navigation scroll position across route changes.
- Updated AHV appliance build guidance for the v1.6.0 release line.

Security and operational notes:
- Upgrade Advisor remains read-only and does not execute LCM updates or mutate
  Nutanix clusters.
- Source Packs should contain curated summaries and references only. Do not
  paste credentials, support portal session data, or unredacted secrets.
