For Cluster A, begin at Start Here and choose the outcome you need. Recover Infrastructure opens recovery preparation or an existing run. Overview brings together managed cluster records, running jobs, fresh PE Good readbacks and open review items. These numbers describe different things; one green indicator cannot establish overall readiness.

An empty managed-cluster list means no explicit record has been registered. It does not prove that your YAML, deployed infrastructure or recovery profiles are missing. Add a reviewed record with its intended endpoint, identity and node baseline, or use Config Files for saved configurations that are not registered clusters.

Open the cluster detail. Review the last PE collection and its age. Refresh reloads saved observations; it does not contact Prism. Collection failed means a read could not be completed. Expired evidence means a recorded result is outside its fifteen-minute window. A clock warning is reported separately.

Review the specific cause, such as certificate trust, an address missing from the certificate, unresolved credentials or connectivity. Correct that cause before collecting again. Do not call the cluster unhealthy merely because the collector could not authenticate or connect.

In Needs attention, open the original cluster or job, then use Update review. Assign an owner, acknowledge with notes or explicitly resolve the item. Acknowledgement does not resolve it. Resolution preserves the original evidence, and a later failed observation can reopen the item.
