Open Unregister PE from PC under Workflows, or its matching Scripts action. Enter the PC endpoint, saved PC credential reference and exact PE cluster UUID. Generating YAML does not contact a target. This administrator-only action has its own guarded job path and is not a general pipeline or scheduled step.

Use Look up target, read-only. This requires strict certificate verification and supported PC APIs; it has no TLS bypass. Review the expected PC UUID and PE name from inventory. Missing, ambiguous or mismatched identities block removal instead of choosing a similar cluster.

Check the version-specific unregistration procedure and dependencies such as replication, networking, application management and self-service configuration. The lookup does not certify those relationships. Record the dependency acknowledgement and have another administrator approve the exact YAML and filename.

Only after validation and exact confirmation does the worker recheck identities, require the vendor dry-run task to succeed and submit one unregister request. An uncertain submission or cancellation can leave remote work running. Inspect and reconcile PC state before requesting a further attempt.

Review the PC task and inventory result against the original UUID. PC-side success does not establish that every PE-side cleanup or application dependency is complete. Follow the documented PE cleanup handoff and site acceptance procedure. Keep the original job and approval as the audit record.
