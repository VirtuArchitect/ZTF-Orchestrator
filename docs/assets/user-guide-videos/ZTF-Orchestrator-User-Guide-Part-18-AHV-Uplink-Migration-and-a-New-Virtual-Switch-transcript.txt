Under Configure, open AHV Uplink Migration and choose PE direct. Prism Central is not required for this path. Enter the expected cluster UUID, PE endpoint and saved PE credential. Provide the CVM credential reference and independently verified SSH host-key fingerprints for every CVM. Keep the required CVM-to-host trust intact.

Certificate verification is enabled by default. If a permitted per-run exception is needed, enter its reason and review the risk: HTTPS remains encrypted, but the certificate no longer authenticates the PE endpoint. The exception is included in the plan, independent approval and audit. It never bypasses SSH host-key checks.

Run read-only discovery and verify identity, complete membership, health and inventory. Choose Create a new virtual switch, such as V S one, or a consistent existing destination. In PE-direct mode, V S one maps to local bridge B R one. For every host, retain at least two operational uplinks on B R zero and two at the destination.

Review cabling, VLANs, MTU, capacity and console access. Run read-only preflight and have a different administrator approve the exact assignments. Live writes default to disabled pending representative hardware validation. A passed draft or mock test is not approval to enable production writes.

When separately validated and enabled, the approved worker processes hosts sequentially and verifies retained management connectivity and both assignments. A failed or uncertain action stops later hosts. Keep completed work recorded and reconcile the actual bridges before a new plan; there is no automatic retry or rollback.
